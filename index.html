Low Earth Orbit (LEO)

A region relatively close to Earth, used by many Earth-observation and communication satellites.

🛰️

What is space debris?

Human-made objects in orbit that no longer serve a useful purpose, including fragments and retired spacecraft.

Protecting space

Better mission planning, responsible disposal and debris mitigation support long-term space sustainability.
